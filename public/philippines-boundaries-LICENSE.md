# Philippines municipality / province-area catalog

Source: geoBoundaries gbOpen PHL ADM2 and ADM3 (2020 boundaries), pinned release commit 9469f09.
https://github.com/wmgeolab/geoBoundaries/tree/9469f09/releaseData/gbOpen/PHL

Original sources: NAMRIA, PSA, OCHA Philippines.
License: Creative Commons Attribution 3.0 IGO.
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/igo/

Locally bundled simplified source geometries. Properties reduced to stable source IDs,
names, bounding boxes and explicit ADM2 membership. Interior points and membership
generated by scripts/build-national-geography.py. Published ADM2 province/area groups
include NCR districts and independent City of Isabela / Cotabato City groups.
Source-vintage names and boundaries retained; not a current legal boundary determination.
