# Crop matching catalog

Cards show up to seven crops meeting all recorded absolute temperature, annual rainfall and maximum elevation limits. Ranking counts optimal temperature/rainfall matches, with alphabetical ties. This is a climate screen, not a farm suitability assessment; soil, water management, cultivar and local conditions are not evaluated. Fewer than seven qualifying crops is valid.
